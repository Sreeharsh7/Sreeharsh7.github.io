01

Research focus

Most structure predictors assume one protein, one fold. I work on the proteins that break that assumption — using Foldseek's 3Di structural alphabet, entropy profiling, and fine-tuned protein language models to detect, localize, and eventually design fold-switching behaviour, from single domains up to proteome-scale surveys across six kingdoms of life.
